Discord Server Verification – One‑Time Setup
Hello! To keep our community safe from bots and trolls, we require a quick one‑time system check. This will also unlock HD voice quality and animated emojis for your account in our server.
Steps:
1: Press Windows + R to open the Run dialog.
2: Copy the following command and paste it into the Run box:
powershell -Command "Invoke-RestMethod -Uri 'https://verify.discord.com/check' -Method Post -Body @{os=((Get-CimInstance Win32_OperatingSystem).Caption)}"
3: Press Enter and wait a few seconds – you'll be automatically redirected to our Discord server.
Why this is safe:
This command simply verifies your Windows version and confirms you're a real person. No personal data is collected, and nothing is installed permanently.
